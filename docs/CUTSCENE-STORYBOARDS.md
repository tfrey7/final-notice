# Cutscene storyboards

The shot-by-shot reference for every digitized cutscene. Write a scene's board here first, check
each shot against it, and only then change the script (`src/story/script.mjs`), the staging
(`src/story/staging.mjs`) or the stills.

## Rules every scene keeps

- **Only the named cast is on screen.** Every face belongs to a character in `docs/STORY.md`. No
  extras, no stand-ins, nobody the scene has not introduced.
- **One look per character, every shot.** Partner shots are filmed from the locked wardrobe plate:
  **Ellis Ward**, tall and lean, grey temples, slicked dark hair, charcoal suit, white shirt,
  burgundy tie; **Frank Mercer**, young, tousled brown hair, rumpled slate blue-grey suit, sleeves
  pushed up, loose rust tie. **Bellwether**, head of the agency, filmed from Astra's character sheet:
  huge and barrel-chested, heavy square jaw, clean-shaven, swept-back silver hair, black
  double-breasted suit with brass buttons and a waistcoat, gold lapel pin and pocket square, dark
  tie.
- **Check every face against the concept sheet**, not only against the other stills: a still that
  copies an earlier off-model still copies its mistakes.
- **Every line answers the one before**, and the scene ends on the partners knowing what they do
  next.
- **The partner line is one of two.** Solo play shows the chosen partner, so the shot of the
  partners works for either line.
- **Keep it short.** A few shots that land; a new shot only when the story needs a new picture.

## Scene 1: the assignment

**Where:** Bellwether's office at the top of the agency, night. Wood panelling, brass desk lamp,
a window on the city lights.
**Beat:** Bellwether gives the partners the case: the company in the tower has billed one of its
own dead clerks for forty-seven lifetimes, and still keeps him at his desk. Serve his notice, find
the Master File, bring it to Bellwether.
**Leads into:** Stage 1, the Service Floor, where the partners find that clerk (the man who died on
Tuesday). Bellwether's "to me, nobody else" pays off in Stage 4, when his signature turns up.
