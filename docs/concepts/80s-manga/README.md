# Final Notice concept art: 1980s anime OVA style

Exploratory concept art (item 2090), not game assets: full resolution, no SNES reduction.
`sheet.jpg` is the six pieces on one page; each piece is beside it at full size.

1. Ward and Mercer, the two caseworkers
2. Bellwether at his window
3. Deputy Director Vellum mid-fight
4. The pyramid-crowned tower at night
5. A Backrooms corridor
6. The Great Seal, gothic cosmic horror

## How they were made

Generated locally on ComfyUI with FLUX.2 [klein] base 4B (Apache-2.0), text prompt only: no LoRA
was used, because no 1980s anime LoRA is installed and none was downloaded. The style comes from
the prompt (80s OVA cel painting, hard shadow shapes, airbrushed backgrounds, neon-on-night
palette, grounded adult proportions). A light pass after generation adds film grain, a soft neon
bloom and a vignette. No paid or online services.

Character looks follow the design pack: Ward tall and thin in charcoal with a red tie, Mercer
shorter and broader in navy with a gold tie, Bellwether compact and silver-haired, Vellum pale in a
black pinstripe suit.
