## The game

A full late-1995 SNES game of six stages, climbing **up** the pyramid-crowned tower. Stages alternate a brawl and an escape climb, and the look rises from corporate through the Backrooms to gothic and cosmic.

| # | Stage | Kind | Look |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | The Service Floor, boss Vellum | brawl | corporate |
| 2 | The Archive, boss the Custodian | escape climb | stale |
| 3 | The middle floors | brawl | Backrooms |
| 4 | The express elevator shaft | escape climb | Backrooms turning wrong |
| 5 | The executive chapel | brawl | gothic |
| 6 | The Great Seal | escape climb into the final boss | cosmic |
